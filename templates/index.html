Safety Engine

Four Possible Outcomes

Every check produces one of four deterministic results — no AI guessing.

GREEN

Current Plan Match

The medicine is already in your discharge prescription at matching strength.

YELLOW

Verify Before Taking

Identified, but not part of your current plan. Consult a pharmacist first.

RED

Professional Review Required

A duplicate ingredient or safety concern was detected. Do not take without review.

GRAY

Unable to Verify

Insufficient information to evaluate. Manual verification is required.

Not a Prescriber

MedSafe never independently prescribes or declares a medicine medically safe. It performs a structured comparison — no more.

Professional Escalation

When uncertainty or duplicate risks are detected, MedSafe immediately directs you to a pharmacist or doctor.

Patient Review First

OCR is an aid — you always review and confirm details before any safety evaluation is run.
