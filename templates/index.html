How MedSafe Works

A straightforward 3-step safety verification for patients at home.

1. Current Prescription

Upload or confirm your hospital discharge medication list.

2. Old Medicine

Scan or enter the medicine you found at home.

3. Safety Check

Deterministic comparison & clear recommended next steps.

Not a Prescriber

MedSafe never independently prescribes or declares a medicine medically safe. It checks for duplicates and matches.

Professional Escalation

When uncertainty or duplicate risks are identified, the system immediately directs the patient to pharmacist or physician review.

Patient Review First

OCR extracts text for assistance, but the patient always reviews and confirms the medicine details before evaluation.
