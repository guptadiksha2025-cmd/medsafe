Ask a Pharmacist

When speaking to your community or hospital pharmacist, consider sharing the following:

Suggested question:
"I was recently discharged from hospital with a new prescription. I found my older {{ check_data.medicine_name }} at home. Can you check whether it is safe to combine with my new medication, or if it's a duplicate?"
  • Bring the original packaging with batch number and expiry date.
  • Bring your printed or digital discharge medication summary.
  • Mention any allergies or other medicines you take.

Contact Prescribing Doctor

Key discussion points for your physician or clinic team:

  • Was {{ check_data.medicine_name }} intentionally discontinued at discharge?
  • Should the dose be adjusted if restarting?
  • Are there known contraindications with your new therapy?
  • Is it safe to take alongside your current medicines?
